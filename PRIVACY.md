# Privacy Policy — Subtitle Focus – Easy-Read Captions

**Effective date:** 23 September 2026

Subtitle Focus – Easy-Read Captions ("the extension") is published by
**Zillionn Ltd** ("we", "us").

- Website: <https://zillionn.com/>
- Contact: <dev@zillionn.com>

## The short version

The extension **does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data.**
It has no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no accounts. Nothing it reads
is ever sent to us or to anyone else. All of its work happens inside your
own browser.

## What the extension does

It finds the subtitles that a video player has already put on the page
and changes how they look (for example, making the start of each word
bold). It does not look at the video picture, the sound or anything else
on the page.

## Information the extension handles, and where it stays

| What | Why | Where it stays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subtitle text shown on the page** | To restyle it. | Only in the page's memory while it is on screen. Never saved and never sent anywhere. |
| **Your settings** (on/off, reading style, emphasis strength, bold weight, colours, and the list of websites where you turned the extension off) | So your choices are remembered. | Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). If you have turned on Chrome Sync, **Chrome** copies these settings to your Google Account so they follow you to your other computers. That is a Chrome feature run by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We never receive these settings. |
| **The "Debug logging" on/off switch** | So the switch is remembered. | Chrome's extension storage on this computer only (`chrome.storage.local`). |
| **Troubleshooting log** (technical notes such as the page address, which player was found and what the extension did) | So you can copy it if you need help. | Only in the tab's memory. It is deleted when you close or reload the tab. It is never sent anywhere automatically. |

## Things that only happen when you click a button

- **"Copy diagnostics" / "Copy logs"** copy troubleshooting information
  (the site's name, the extension's status and version, and, for "Copy
  logs", the troubleshooting log) to your clipboard. Nothing is sent
  anywhere. You decide whether to paste it somewhere.
- **"Report this site"** opens a new tab on GitHub (github.com) with a
  bug-report form. The form is filled in with the website's name (for
  example `example.com`), the extension's status and its version number.
  **Nothing is sent until you read the form and choose to submit it** on
  GitHub, which needs a GitHub account. If you submit it, the report is
  public on GitHub and [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
  applies.
- **"Support development"** opens Zillionn's tip page on Buy Me a Coffee
  (buymeacoffee.com) in a new tab. The extension itself sends nothing. If
  you choose to leave a tip, the payment is handled by Buy Me a Coffee and
  its payment provider, Stripe, under their own privacy policies. We
  receive only what Buy Me a Coffee shares with page owners (such as the
  name, email address and message you give when tipping), and never your
  card details. Tipping is entirely optional and doesn't change anything
  in the extension.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- **Read and change data on the websites you visit** (`<all_urls>`):
  subtitles can appear on any website, so the extension needs to be able
  to find and restyle them on whatever site you watch video on. It only
  starts working when a video is playing, and you can turn it off for any
  site from its popup.
- **Storage** (`storage`): to save your settings, as described above.

## What we never do

- The extension never collects or sends us any personal information,
  browsing history, subtitle text or page content. (The only personal
  details we ever receive are the ones you choose to give when leaving an
  optional tip, as described above.)
- We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.
- We do not use or transfer data for advertising, credit checks, lending
  or any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
- The extension does not download or run code from the internet.

The use of information received by the extension follows the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Children

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a
future version ever needs to handle your data differently, we will tell
you clearly inside the extension and, where required, ask for your
permission **before** the change takes effect.
